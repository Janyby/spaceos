View — live page frame via CDP (Page.captureScreenshot, ~1.5 s), no mimicry and no security policy bypass. Clicks inside the page are not forwarded by this contract — interactivity happens in the Chromium window via «Show in Chromium» or through the built-in iframe surface (`#bframe`) when loaded.
